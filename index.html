Bukmark

Bookmark your favorites — with your own names.

Save posts from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Threads, X and LinkedIn, give each one a nickname you'll remember, and find it again in a second.

  • Nicknames — swap long titles for names you actually search for.
  • Collections — colourful shelves for recipes, inspo, wishlists.
  • Instant search — by nickname, account or collection.
  • No account — everything lives on your phone; back up and move with one file.

Pomeowdoro

A Pomodoro timer with a cat.

A tiny retro desktop timer that fits on one little screen. Start the clock, and a pixel cat naps, wakes and chases your cursor while you focus.

  • Focus cycles — any length; click the digits and type 30 or 12:30.
  • A cat that plays — hover the screen and it chases your cursor.
  • Focus music — binaural beats, brown noise and rain.
  • Task paper — a note that slides away as time runs out.
Windows 10 / 11 · free · single .exe · no install
